## What You Do (All on One Page):

### 📝 Step 1: Add Your Writing
- **Just paste** your writing samples into the text box
- Stories, emails, blog posts - anything in your style
- The more text, the better (aim for 2,000+ characters)

### 🚀 Step 2: Train Your AI
- Click the **"Train AI"** button
- Watch the progress bar
- Wait a minute or two while it learns
- Adjust settings with sliders if you want (optional)

### ✍️ Step 3: Generate New Text
- Type what you want written (like "Once upon a time...")
- Click **"Generate Text"**
- See text written in YOUR style!
- Click **"Copy"** to use it
